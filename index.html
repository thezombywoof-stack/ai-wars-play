Each round: ① New Round button (slides river & refills all empty slots; slots give −0/−0/−0/−1/−2). ② Builder first, alternate 2 actions each: Acquire a river card · Agenda (draw 1 OR play 1 from hand) · Grind (take 2⚡ or 2🗳) · Steer (pay 3 any mix, choose: Align +1 · Trust +1 · Doom −1 (Steward also gets Trust +1 with this choice) · Cap +1 — Builder only). ③ Income from tableau cards. ④ Doom Check button: Cap>Align → Doom+1; Cap≥8 → Doom+1; total Model Risk ≥ 7 → Doom+1; Power Draw: each player with 3+ ⚡ income → Trust −1 (the grid remembers).

Only its tuck bar applies; VP kept; permanent; discarded with host. ▶Opp: lines fire on your opponent's actions. Leaders: one active each; recruiting a new one exiles the old.
